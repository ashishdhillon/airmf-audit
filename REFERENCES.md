## Governance & Compliance Standards
- **NIST AI 100-1**: *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, National Institute of Standards and Technology, 2023. Core functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage.
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023**: *Information Technology — Artificial Intelligence — Management System (AIMS)*.
- **EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689)**: Technical documentation, transparency obligations, and cybersecurity requirements for general-purpose AI models.
